Not yet scheduled Castings that have a Casting Inventory in the portal and no days on the grid yet. Hours come from the casting's Optimizer Hours tab. Schedule = spread those tasks from the cast date.
How the spread works Each phase on the Optimizer Hours tab becomes one task. Days per phase = hours ÷ the department's hours-per-day (Settings). Hours are split evenly, so the grid always totals exactly the Optimizer Hours. The Cast phase lands on the cast date; earlier phases run back from it, later ones forward. Weekends are skipped unless you allow one; holidays are never used.
